I'd like to propose an important research direction for crypto governance: developing a UBI token model as a metric for ecosystem health.

The premise is straightforward but powerful: governanceHealth correlates directly with participants' ability to sustain themselves economically within the protocol. When contributors, validators, and community members can make a living from their ecosystem participation, it signals robust, equitable value distribution.

A UBI token framework could serve as both measurement tool and design principle:

**As a Metric:**
- Track the minimum viable network scale needed to support basic income for participants
- Measure value distribution across different governance models
- Identify critical thresholds where ecosystems become self-sustaining

**As a Design Principle:**
- Use governance mechanisms for fair exclusion rather than concentration
- Design token economics that scale sustainably with network growth
- Create feedback loops between participation and livelihood

This approach would give us concrete data on:
- What network effects are required for different UBI modes (partial, full, conditional)
- How governance structures affect economic sustainability
- Where bottlenecks emerge in value distribution

The most interesting aspect: governance-as-exclusion-mechanism. Rather than optimizing for token holder concentration, we could design systems where governance actively prevents value capture, ensuring broad-based economic participation.

I believe this research could bridge idealistic UBI goals with practical tokenomics, giving builders concrete targets for sustainable, equitable protocol design.

Would love to connect with researchers, protocol designers, and governance practitioners interested in exploring this framework. What metrics would you prioritize in measuring governance health through economic sustainability?
